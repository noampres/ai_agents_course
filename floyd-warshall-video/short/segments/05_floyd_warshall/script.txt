Floyd-Warshall asks a different question: not how many edges, but which vertices a path may pass through.
Round k: is going through k shorter? One comparison per cell.
Start with k equals A.
B to D: eight plus seven is fifteen, better than infinity.
C to B: five plus three is eight, also better.
D to B: two plus three is five, better again.
Round B finds two more shortcuts at once.
Round C finds three more at once.
And round D fixes the last three.
And we land on exactly the same table, in n cubed time.
